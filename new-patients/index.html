What to Expect as a New Patient

We understand visiting a new dental office can be intimidating. Here's what you can expect during your first visit with us.

Upon Arrival

Our friendly front desk team will welcome you with a smile and guide you through the check-in process:

  • Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment
  • Bring your ID, insurance information, and completed new patient forms (or fill them out upon arrival)
  • Our team will verify your information and answer any initial questions
  • Enjoy our comfortable waiting area with complimentary beverages

We strive to minimize wait times and ensure a pleasant experience from the moment you walk through our doors.

During Your Exam

Your first dental exam with us will be thorough and informative:

  • Review of your medical and dental history
  • Full set of digital X-rays to identify any issues not visible during visual examination
  • Comprehensive oral examination of teeth, gums, and surrounding tissues
  • Oral cancer screening for your peace of mind
  • Assessment of your bite and jaw alignment
  • Professional cleaning by our gentle hygienist (unless a different treatment plan is needed)

Our dentist will explain findings in easy-to-understand terms and answer all your questions.

Treatment Planning

After your examination, we'll create a personalized plan for your ongoing care:

  • Discussion of findings and recommended treatments
  • Visual aids and digital images to help you understand any dental issues
  • Prioritization of treatments based on urgency and your preferences
  • Transparent cost estimates and insurance coverage information
  • Scheduling of follow-up appointments as needed

We believe in collaborative decision-making – you'll never feel pressured into treatments. Our goal is to provide you with the information you need to make confident choices about your dental health.

Have Additional Questions?

We're here to make your transition to our practice as smooth as possible. Contact our friendly team for any additional questions or to schedule your first appointment.
